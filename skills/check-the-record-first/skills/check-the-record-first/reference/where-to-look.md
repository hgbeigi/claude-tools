# Where to look

Fill this in once, then keep it current. Claude reads it, in this order, before stating a fact about your life or asking you a question. Use paths from your project folder, or full paths for folders elsewhere on your computer.

Delete the example rows when you add your own.

## Subjects

| Subject | Where the records are | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Health | `records/health/` | One file per person. Read the whole file, not the summary. |
| Money and accounts | `records/money/accounts.md` | Which account pays which bill. |
| Insurance | `records/insurance/` | Policies, renewal dates, claims. |
| Home | `records/home/` | Equipment, repairs, warranties. |
| Car | `records/car/` | Service history, registration dates. |

## Decisions

Where settled decisions are written: `notes/decisions.md`

## Past sessions

Where notes from earlier sessions are kept: `notes/`

## Never ask about

Facts that are always in the records, so Claude should never ask you for them:

- Example: the names and birthdays of people in the household
- Example: which bank pays the mortgage
